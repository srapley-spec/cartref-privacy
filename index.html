Cartref Home privacy

Effective 17 November 2026. Cartref Home is a beta home-control app provided by Sam Rapley. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact sam.rapley@me.com. This policy describes Cartref; your Home Assistant administrator and connected music services also control their own data.

Connecting to your home

Your chosen Home Assistant server receives sign-in requests and commands. Cartref stores authentication tokens in iOS Keychain and web session data in its app storage. The app and its extensions share credentials through an entitled Keychain group. Server settings, selected rooms and cached home/media state are stored locally, including an App Group shared with widgets and system media controls. Credentials are not included in push notifications.

Home and music information

Cartref reads authorized room, device, alarm, routine, media and account presence information from Home Assistant to display your home and contextual controls. Presence is obtained from your existing home configuration; Cartref does not independently track your GPS location. Artwork may be fetched through Home Assistant or from the artwork provider. Music listening uses Apple Music authorization and system playback services where enabled; Apple and the originating service process those requests under their policies.

Background updates

An optional administrator-operated relay stores device identifiers, Home Assistant account identifiers, selected rooms and Apple push tokens to deliver background updates. Apple Push Notification service processes these deliveries. Remote music updates contain playback metadata; widget refresh notifications request a refresh without carrying home presence data. The relay runs on the home administrator’s computer. Registration records are retained until an authenticated removal or administrator deletion; they do not currently expire automatically.

Storage and your choices

Local settings and cached state remain until cleared or the app is removed; Keychain items may survive app removal. Reset web session clears web data but is not a complete deletion or server-session revocation. Revoke Home Assistant sessions through your server account settings and contact the home administrator to remove relay registrations or retained home data. You can revoke Music access in iOS Settings. For help removing native connection data, contact the provider above. Home Assistant and music providers determine their own server retention.

Demo and diagnostics

Explore demo uses temporary fictional home data, does not sign in or send home commands, and resets when you exit or reset the demo. Cartref includes no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK. Apple processes TestFlight installation, crash and feedback information under its TestFlight privacy terms and shares beta feedback with the developer. Avoid including credentials or private home details in feedback.
